Frame annotation

You will see {{ n_items }} still frames from short videos, one at a time. For each frame, choose the category that best describes what is visible in the image.

  • Weapons: firearms, missiles, artillery or other weapons are visible.
  • Military aircraft / maritime / ground vehicles: a military aircraft, ship or boat, or ground vehicle (tank, armoured vehicle, military truck) is visible.
  • Non-military: none of the above, including civilian planes, boats and cars, toys and video-game footage unless it depicts real military equipment.
  • Use Unsure if you cannot decide, and Frame missing if no image is shown.

Work independently; do not discuss items with other annotators. Your answers are saved after each item and you can stop and resume at any time.
